# Examples

Each page takes one of the [sample datasets](../guide/sample-data) and shows it several ways, with the layers that suit
it best. Every example runs where it stands: press **Run**, then **Edit** to change it. They use small sizes so they
start quickly; the [tech demo](../demo) goes up to millions of rows.

| Page | The data | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Rides in New York](./rides) | Ride pickups and drop-offs | Points, heatmaps, rush hour against late night, trips as arcs, 3D hexagons, the day played back |
| [Flights](./flights) | Routes between 96 airports | Great circles, airports by traffic, one hub, long-haul routes, airlines, departures through the day |
| [Delivery fleet](./fleet) | GPS traces over a working day | Trips played back, whole routes, where vehicles spend their time |
| [Earthquakes](./earthquakes) | 25 years along the plate boundaries | Magnitude and depth, the big ones, the Ring of Fire in 3D, the years played back |
| [Ships](./ships) | Positions on the shipping lanes | Lanes by ship type, speed near ports, the busiest waters, tankers only |
| [Districts in Chicago](./districts) | Census-tract-like polygons | Income, density in 3D, long commutes |
| [Supply chain](./supply-chain) | A graph in smaltcy | The network, what reaches one city, one tier at a time |
